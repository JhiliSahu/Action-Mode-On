# Maze Craze

> **[▶ Play Maze Craze](https://jhilisahu.github.io/Action-Mode-On/)**<br>
> The game will be available at this link after GitHub Pages is enabled for the repository.

Find your way through three first-person neon labyrinths. Explore with a fog-of-war map, collect five golden keys in each maze, and reach the magical finish portal. There is **no timer**—take all the time you need.

## How to Play

1. Select **Enter the Maze** on the title screen.
2. Explore the corridors. Nearby passages are revealed on your map as you go.
3. Find all five golden keys to unlock the finish portal.
4. Reach the portal to complete the maze and continue to the next one.
5. Finish all three mazes to escape.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Move forward / backward | `W` / `S` or `↑` / `↓` |
| Turn left / right | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| Pause or resume | `P` or `Esc` |
| Restart | Choose **Restart Maze** or **Restart Run** from the pause menu |

## What to Expect

- Three increasingly challenging first-person neon mazes
- Five collectible, golden lock keys in every maze
- A map that reveals nearby corridors as you explore
- A magical portal that unlocks when all five keys are collected
- No countdown or time limit

## Tips

- Check your map often to keep track of explored corridors.
- Use turns deliberately at intersections; the maze has many dead ends.
- Pause any time with `P` or `Esc`. Your progress is safe while paused.
